Our story

At SMOOR, everything begins with a simple thought of, “how can we make this moment feel a little more special?” That’s what drives us. From handpicked ingredients to thoughtful craftsmanship, we pour care into everything we create. Not just to satisfy a craving, but to add a touch of meaning to the moments you choose to celebrate. Big or small, planned or spontaneous; here’s to making it all a little more memorable. Celebrate More with SMOOR!

2018

SMOOR becomes the first in India to launch Gold Chocolate, setting a new benchmark in luxury indulgence.

2019

Introduced Ruby Chocolate — the rare fourth type of chocolate — to Indian chocolate lovers. 

Extended support during the pandemic by distributing meals to frontline doctors. 

2021

Named Brand of the Year in the Chocolates category at the Channelier FMCG Awards.

2022

Acquired by Rebel Foods, joining forces to scale the art of fine chocolate across India. 

Unveiled our 50th store — the flagship Signature Lounge at Lavelle Road. 

2023

Won the tender to launch a Bakery Café at Bengaluru International Airport’s Terminal 2.

2024

Secured a landmark ₹2.5 crore corporate order from Titan.

 Announced an exclusive collaboration with Baileys to create non-alcoholic Baileys-flavoured treats and desserts. 

2025

Opened doors to India’s largest chocolate destination — a 65,000 sq. ft. SMOOR Experience Centre redefining how the country experiences indulgence.

About

SMOOR is a premium brand crafting couverture chocolates, desserts, and gourmet experiences. Blending global techniques with Indian roots, we use the finest ingredients to create indulgences that celebrate quality and craftsmanship. From handcrafted treats to curated hampers, every creation is made to elevate moments, big or small.
