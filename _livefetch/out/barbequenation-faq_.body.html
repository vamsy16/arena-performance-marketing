You can upload your resume to https://www.barbequenation.com/career/. Our team will reach you if we find any suitable opening.

What is the Smile Coins program and how do I join?

+
It is our loyalty program where 1 Smile Coin = ₹1. You join automatically by creating an account on our App or Website, instantly starting as a Silver member with a free 100 Smile Coins welcome bonus!

How do I make sure I earn Smile Coins on my bill?

+
To get your tier's cashback (8%, 10%, or 12%), either your reservation must be made through the Barbeque Nation App/Website, or your final bill payment must be processed through the app/website.

How do I earn Smiles Coins?

+
You can earn SMILES Coins on dine-in transactions where the dine-in reservation is made through the Barbeque Nation App/website, or the bill is settled through our app or the payment link.

How do I redeem my Smiles Coins?

+
You can redeem your SMILES Coins within 120 days from the date they got credited to your account. To redeem your SMILES Coins, log in to Barbeque Nation through your mobile app or website and book a table. During check out, you will find an option to add SMILES Coins and reserve a table. Barbeque Nation management retains the authority to amend the policy governing the acquisition, redemption, and expiry of SMILES without prior notification.

Is there a Signup bonus ?

+
There is a sign up bonus of 100 SMILES Coins which can be redeemed to settle the bill when you dine- in with us

Is there a referral program ?

+
The referral program is no longer active. Effective date (14th June)

What happens to my Smile Coins if I cancel my table reservation?

+
If you cancel your reservation before your scheduled reservation time, all the Smile Coins you used will be instantly refunded to your account. If you cancel after your reservation time has passed, the coins cannot be refunded.
