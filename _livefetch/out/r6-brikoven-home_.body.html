At Brik Oven, we serve the iconic and original Neapolitan-style pizza.

These delicious handmade pizzas have a light and crunchy crust that’s thin in the centre and thick and charred around the edges, baked in a rustic wood-fired oven at 400 C using a traditional recipe from Naples. Its wholesome flavor comes from a combination of carefully selected toppings on a sauce made with organic San Marzano tomatoes.

The sourdough base we use packs almost a decade of flavour. Fermented for 48-72 hours (depending on the season) , the wild yeast creates a light and fluffy dough that gives you a characteristic flavour and taste.

Things you might not know about us

 
 

We make our own cheese every morning.

At Brik Oven we source fresh cows milk every morning. This milk is quality checked and tested by our experienced chefs before turn it into fresh mozzarella, which then gets shipped to all our outlets giving you the tastiest and freshest pizzas available in all of India.

We bring our wood fired oven to you.

Yes you heard correct. We bring a portable oven to your desired location which is fired completely with wood. We will have skilled chefs present at the event stretching and spinning the dough by hand,  making every pizza fresh right out of a blazing 400 degree oven. The menu for every individual event is set by the Chefs keeping in mind the host’s requirements. Contact Us to book your event now !!
