# My Analysis Skill Stack - For Performance Marketing Lead Audit

When you give me a brand URL, I run it through 7 skills - same as a senior media buyer + CRO + creative strategist would do.

---

### SKILL 1: AD INTELLIGENCE (Media Buying Skill)
**Tools I use:** Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency, LinkedIn Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center
**What I find:**
- How many active ads they have?
- How long are they running? (If ad runs >14 days = it's working, but maybe not optimized)
- Which platforms are they spending on?
- Are they doing retargeting or only cold?
- Budget signals: 5 ads = testing, 20+ ads = scaling but likely wasting

**What it tells you:** Is this lead spending real money or just boosting posts?

### SKILL 2: CREATIVE STRATEGY (Creative Director Skill)
**What I check:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds (for video)
- UGC vs Studio vs Static vs Founder face?
- Pattern interrupt, subtitles, aspect ratios (1:1, 4:5, 9:16)
- Creative diversity: Do they have 10 angles or same image 10 times?
- Ad fatigue signals

**What it tells you:** Your opening line for outreach - "You are running 12 static ads, zero UGC. Your competitor scaled with UGC..."

### SKILL 3: DIRECT RESPONSE COPYWRITING
**What I check:**
- Angle: Discount / Pain / Desire / Social Proof / USP?
- Structure: Hook -> Pain -> Solution -> CTA?
- Weakness: Too long? No CTA? No urgency?
- Compliance: Are they making claims that get ad account banned?

**What it tells you:** How to pitch better angles they are NOT using.

### SKILL 4: FUNNEL & CRO AUDIT (Conversion Rate Optimization Skill)
**Tools I use:** Fetch website, PageSpeed Insights logic, Mobile audit, BuiltWith tech check
**What I check:**
- Above-the-fold: Is offer clear in 3 seconds?
- Speed: Does site load in <3 sec on mobile? (Every 1 sec delay = 7% drop)
- Trust: Reviews, UGC, trust badges, COD, return policy?
- Friction: Long form? No sticky ATC? No pop-up for email?
- Offer: Weak "Buy Now" vs Strong "Bundle + Free Gift + Guarantee"

**What it tells you:** The 3 quick wins you can promise to fix.

### SKILL 5: TRACKING & ANALYTICS (Tech Skill)
**What I check:**
- Is Meta Pixel installed? Google Tag? TikTok Pixel?
- Is CAPI / Server-side tracking there?
- Is GA4 configured properly?
- Are they capturing emails before checkout?
- Are they doing retargeting? (If no retargeting ad found = HUGE leak)

**What it tells you:** 70% of Indian D2C brands have broken tracking after iOS14. This is your biggest value prop.

### SKILL 6: COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE
**Tools I use:** Web Search, SimilarWeb, Ad Library comparison
**What I find:**
- Who are their top 2-3 competitors?
- What creatives are competitors using that they are not?
- What offers are competitors running?

**What it tells you:** FOMO angle - "Your competitor XYZ is doing this..."

### SKILL 7: OUTREACH & SALES PSYCHOLOGY (Your Closer Skill)
**What I do:**
- I convert all findings into 3 outreach angles:
  1. **Roast Audit:** "Found 3 leaks costing you money..."
  2. **Value-First:** Loom video script with quick win
  3. **FOMO:** "Your competitor is doing X, you are not..."

This is not "Hi we are performance agency" - This is "I researched YOU specifically"
