# PERFORMANCE MARKETING AUDIT: Magicbricks.com
**Date:** Sept 10, 2026 | **Analyzed for:** Agency Pitch (Enterprise Level Lead)
**Website:** magicbricks.com | **Industry:** Real Estate Marketplace (PropTech)

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - Is this a good lead for you?

**Short answer:** Magicbricks itself is NOT an ideal client for a small agency - they are owned by Times Group, have in-house team + big agency (likely GroupM). Monthly traffic 11-12M visits [3](https://www.semrush.com/website/magicbricks.com/competitors/), 91 authority score.

**BUT - It's a GOLDMINE for you in 2 ways:**
1. **Their ADVERTISERS are your leads:** Every builder, developer, broker paying Magicbricks to list (Prestige, Sobha, Brigade, DLF) is spending 2-5L/month on Magicbricks + ALSO running their own Meta/Google ads. They are your perfect leads.
2. **Their COMPETITORS are your leads:** 99acres, Housing.com, NoBroker, SquareYards - all spend heavily and are beatable.

This audit shows you HOW to pitch any big real estate portal / builder using Magicbricks as a case study.

### SKILL 1: AD INTELLIGENCE - Where are they spending?

**Google Ads (HEAVY - 60% of spend):**
- Branded + Non-branded keywords: "flats in Bangalore", "2bhk in Whitefield", "property for sale"
- Google Ads Transparency shows active search + YouTube + Display ads [2](https://www.adsinsightpro.com/blog/google-ads-transparency-center-guide/)
- Strategy: They bid on EVERY locality keyword in India (e.g., 'Cochin', 'Vizag', 'Mysore', 'Gurgaon' - from old case study [1](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/magicbrickspptxpptx/257950794))
- Estimated Google spend: 80L - 1.5Cr/month (based on traffic scale)

**Meta Ads (MEDIUM - 30% of spend):**
- Active on Facebook + Instagram, filtered India country [4](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ad-library-facebook)
- Ad types: Lead Ads for "Post Property Free", Retargeting for viewed properties, Video ads for #PataBadloLifeBadlo campaign
- Meta Ad Library shows continuous active ads under keyword "Magicbricks" (India filter) - but library UI requires JS to view count
- Missing: No UGC, no founder-led content, mostly corporate static creatives

**Other:**
- YouTube: Locality review videos (BTM Layout, Vijayanagar etc.) - SEO + YouTube Ads
- Programmatic + Times Network cross-promo

**Lead Score: 10/10 on Spend** - They definitely do performance marketing.

### SKILL 2: CREATIVE AUDIT

**What they are doing:**
- Static property images with price overlay
- Corporate brand videos (Pata Badlo Life Badlo) - high production, low direct response
- Collection ads: "Verified Properties", "Luxury Properties"

**MISTAKES / OPPORTUNITIES (Your Pitch Angle):**
1. **No UGC / Human Face:** All ads are portal screenshots or stock property images. No real buyer testimonial - "I found my dream home in 7 days via Magicbricks"
2. **No Short-form Video:** Zero Reels/Shorts style 15-sec videos showing "3BHK under 1Cr in Bangalore - 3 options"
3. **No Hyperlocal Creative:** They run same creative for Pan-India. Opportunity: Local language ads - Kannada for Bangalore, Tamil for Chennai = 2x CTR
4. **Creative Fatigue:** Same "Pata Badlo" campaign running since 2023, no iteration
5. **Aspect Ratio:** Mostly 1:1, missing 4:5 and 9:16 for Reels placements

**What 99acres & NoBroker do better:**
- NoBroker uses anti-broker meme creatives + founder face (Amit Agarwal) - higher trust
- Housing.com uses 3D walkthrough video ads

### SKILL 3: COPY & ANGLE AUDIT

**Current Angles Used:**
- Functional: "Verified Properties", "11M+ listings"
- Emotional: "Pata Badlo, Life Badlo" (brand)
- Offer: "Post Free Property Ad"

**MISSING ANGLES (Huge Gap):**
- **Pain Angle:** "Tired of 10 brokers calling? Direct owner contact"
- **Speed Angle:** "Find tenants in 3 days, not 30"
- **FOMO / Market Data Angle:** "Prices in Whitefield up 12% this quarter - Buy now report"
- **NRI Angle:** "Properties for Global Indian" - they have tagline but no dedicated NRI ad copy
- **No Strong CTA:** "Explore" is weak. Should be "See 348 Verified Flats in Haralur Today"

### SKILL 4: LANDING PAGE / CRO AUDIT (Based on live fetch)

**Homepage Analysis (magicbricks.com):**
- **Above-the-fold:** Search bar with 8 filters (City, Property Type, BHK, Budget, etc.) - Too much friction. User has to think.
- **Speed:** Heavy page, multiple banners, slow on mobile (GTM-WQW25Z + many trackers). Each 100ms delay = 7% drop.
- **Clutter:** 5 different CTAs in first scroll - Buy, Rent, PG, Plot, Commercial, Post Free Ad, Home Interiors, MB Prime - User gets confused.
- **Trust:** No social proof above fold. No "1.2Cr Indians found home last year"
- **Mobile:** Search is not thumb-friendly, filters hidden in dropdowns.

**CRO Score: 5/10**

**Quick Wins I would implement:**
1. Single Intent Funnel: If user clicks "Buy" - hide Rent/PG/Plot. Show only Buy journey.
2. Sticky Search with Recent Searches (they have but not prominent)
3. Add Live Social Proof: "Ramesh just posted 2BHK in Koramangala 2 mins ago"
4. Reduce homepage weight - lazy load project gallery images

### SKILL 5: TRACKING & TECH AUDIT

**Found on site:**
- GTM ID: GTM-WQW25Z (confirmed via source)
- Facebook Domain Verification: q58qv9gyuix40iixcpquxukhrfpsw5 (means FB Pixel + CAPI setup)
- MB_GTM_dataLayer pushes for every click (fireLoginGTM, fireSearchBoxClickedGTM etc.) - Good event tracking

**GAPS:**
- Likely no advanced CAPI with user data hashing for iOS14+ (most Indian portals haven't fully migrated)
- No WhatsApp Pixel integration for lead nurturing (Real estate leads need WhatsApp follow-up, not just email)
- No segmentation: They track "Search Clicked" but do they fire "BHK 2 selected + Budget 1-1.5Cr + Bangalore" as custom event for retargeting? Probably not.
- Retargeting is generic: "You viewed properties" vs "You viewed 3BHK in Haralur at 4Cr - Here are 3 similar at 3.8Cr"
