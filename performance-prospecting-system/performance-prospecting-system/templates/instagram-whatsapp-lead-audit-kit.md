# AUDIT KIT: For Leads With NO Website (Instagram / WhatsApp Diversion)
**Common in:** Local businesses, Salons, Clinics, Real Estate Brokers, Coaches, Small D2C, Jewellery, Fashion boutiques

In Meta Ad Library, if you see:
- CTA = "Send WhatsApp Message" or "Visit Instagram Profile" or "Send Message"
- No display link (no website URL shown)
= This is Instagram/WhatsApp lead.

You CANNOT use website CRO audit. Use this instead.

---

### HOW TO IDENTIFY DESTINATION IN AD LIBRARY:

1. Open ad in Ad Library
2. Look at CTA button:
   - "Shop Now" / "Learn More" = Website (normal audit)
   - "Send WhatsApp Message" = WhatsApp funnel
   - "Visit Instagram Profile" / No button but profile visible = Instagram funnel
   - "Send Message" = Messenger funnel

### SKILL STACK FOR INSTAGRAM / WHATSAPP LEADS (Modified)

#### SKILL 1: AD INTELLIGENCE (Same)
- How many active ads?
- How long running? (If WhatsApp ad running >21 days = it's profitable for them, but they are losing 60% leads due to no automation)
- Creative: Still check UGC vs static

#### SKILL 2: INSTAGRAM PROFILE AUDIT (Replaces Landing Page Audit)

This is your CRO audit. Instagram IS their website.

**Checklist - Score 0-10:**

**Bio (First 3 seconds):**
- [ ] Is it clear what they do + for whom + where? (e.g., "Skin Clinic in Koramangala | Acne Treatment | 5000+ patients")
- [ ] Is there a clear CTA + link? (Linktree with booking link or WhatsApp link?)
- [ ] Is location + contact in bio?

**Highlights (This is their menu):**
- [ ] Do they have highlights? (Services, Reviews, Results, Price, Location, FAQ)
- [ ] Are highlight covers professional or random?
- [ ] Most leads have ZERO highlights or only "2023" - HUGE opportunity

**Feed (Trust & Proof):**
- [ ] Pinned posts: Do they have 3 pinned posts for Offer / Proof / How to book?
- [ ] Recent 9 posts: Is it all random or structured? (Before/After, Testimonials, Offers, BTS)
- [ ] Reels: Are they posting Reels? How many views? (If no Reels = opportunity)
- [ ] Social proof: Comments, likes, tagged posts?

**Story + DM Funnel:**
- [ ] Are they active on Stories daily?
- [ ] Do they have Story Highlights with "Book Now" sticker?
- [ ] Auto-reply in DM? (You can test by messaging "Hi")

**Score:**
- 0-3 = Very weak Instagram = HOT LEAD (easy to pitch)
- 4-6 = Average = Good lead
- 7-10 = Strong = Harder to pitch, but you can pitch ads scaling

#### SKILL 3: WHATSAPP FUNNEL AUDIT (If CTA = WhatsApp)

**Test it yourself - Click ad -> Send "Hi" on WhatsApp:**

**Check:**
1. **Response Time:** Do they reply in <5 mins or after 5 hours? (If slow = losing 80% leads)
2. **Auto Greeting:** Is there a WhatsApp Business greeting message? Catalog?
3. **Catalog:** Do they have WhatsApp Catalog with products/services + prices?
4. **Quick Replies:** Do they use quick replies or typing manually every time?
5. **Follow-up:** If you don't reply, do they follow up after 1 day?
6. **Payment Link:** Can they collect payment on WhatsApp or they say "GPay to this number"?

**Common Leaks:**
- No WhatsApp Business API (using personal number)
- No auto-reply after 10pm (leads come at night)
- No saved catalog (sending images one by one)
- No CRM - leads lost in chat
- No retargeting - they don't have pixel because no website, so can't retarget

**Your Opportunity:**
- Setup WhatsApp Business API + Auto greeting + Catalog + Quick replies
- Setup landing page to capture lead + then push to WhatsApp (so you can pixel it)
- Setup abandoned chat follow-up automation

#### SKILL 4: TRACKING AUDIT (For IG/WhatsApp leads)

**Reality:** They have NO tracking.
- No Pixel (because no website)
- No way to retarget website visitors
- No way to know which ad gave sale
- Running ads blindly

**Your Pitch:** "You are spending 30k/month but you don't know which ad got you client. With a simple 1-page landing page + pixel, we can track and cut your cost per lead by 40%"

#### SKILL 5: CREATIVE & COPY (Same as before, but more important)

For WhatsApp/IG leads, creative is EVERYTHING because there's no website to convince - only ad -> chat.

- Are they using same static poster for all ads?
- Is hook strong? "Acne gone in 7 days" vs "Welcome to our clinic"
- Do they have video testimonials? (Most don't)

#### SKILL 6: COMPETITOR BENCHMARK

Find 2 competitors in same area who HAVE website + good Instagram + WhatsApp catalog. Show side-by-side.

Example: "Your competitor - Dr. Batra's Koramangala - has Instagram with 3 pinned posts + highlights + booking link + 24 active ads. You have 2 ads and no highlights."
