### PART 2: What I Will Analyze For Each Lead (My 9-Point Audit)

For every lead you give me, I will deliver this:

**1. Active Presence Check**
- Are they running on Meta? Google? YouTube? LinkedIn?
- Estimated ad activity (active ads count from Ad Library)
- Funnel type: Lead Gen, Website Traffic, Shopify Store, App Install?

**2. Creative Audit**
- What creatives are they using? (UGC, static, founder video, testimonial?)
- Creative mistakes: Bad hook in first 3 sec, no subtitles, poor design, no variation
- Creative fatigue signals

**3. Copy & Angle Audit**
- What angle are they using? (Discount, fear, social proof, feature?)
- Copy mistakes: No CTA, too long, no pain point
- What angle they are NOT using that could work

**4. Landing Page / Website CRO Audit**
- Speed, mobile responsiveness
- Above-the-fold clarity, CTA, trust badges, reviews
- Form length, friction, pixel check

**5. Offer & Positioning Audit**
- Is their offer weak? (e.g., just "50% off" vs strong bundle/guarantee)
- How they are positioned vs 3 competitors

**6. Tracking & Retargeting Audit**
- Is Meta Pixel / Google Tag installed?
- Are they doing retargeting? (I can check via ad library patterns)
- Missed opportunity: No remarketing, no email capture

**7. Competitor Benchmark**
- What are top 2 competitors in same niche doing better?

**8. 3 Quick Wins (This is your outreach gold)**
- 3 specific, actionable improvements they can do in next 7 days
- Example: "Your landing page has no reviews above fold, adding 3 video testimonials can increase CVR by 20-30%"

**9. Personalized Outreach Angle**
- Based on audit, I will write you:
  a) Loom video script (30 sec)
  b) Cold DM / Email - 3 variations (Short, Value-first, Roast-style)
  c) Subject lines
