*Under 90 words. No deck, no call request.*

> Hi <Name>, I was looking at <Brand>'s ads on Meta Ad Library — saw <X> creatives running for <> weeks.
>
> Quick observation: the ads don't lead with the ₹78,000 subsidy and the CTA goes to a form instead of WhatsApp. In rooftop solar that combination usually costs 25–40% of enquiries.
